## 3. Nhung gi thay doi vi 3 quyet dinh tren

### Vi gia = CO

- `Product` + `Offer` co `price` + `priceCurrency: VND` + `availability` -> du dieu kien
  product snippet, tuc co the hien gia tren SERP dung nhu doi thu #1
- **PHAI ghi ro don vi gia** - day la lo hong cua doi thu: ho ghi "19.000 d" khong ghi
  /kg hay /bao. Ta ghi ro `19.000d/kg` (hoac dung so that) la vuot ngay
- `priceValidUntil` phai duoc set va gia han. Doi thu de het han tu 2025-10-10
- Sinh ra mot **nghia vu van hanh vinh vien**: cap nhat gia hang thang, dong bo giua
  HTML va JSON-LD. Phai co nguoi chiu trach nhiem, neu khong thi gia sai con te hon
  khong co gia
- **KHONG lam AggregateRating gia.** Dung copy nuoc di 5 sao / 1 review tu viet cua
  trungnamcons

### Vi muc tieu = ca hai, uu tien ban le

- Giu huong 42 trang quy cach (hung long-tail) - da xac nhan an toan theo cong
  Scaled Content Abuse vi 42 < 100 va spec khac nhau that
- Moi trang quy cach phai co **2 duong ra**: mua le (gia + don vi + CTA dat hang)
  va **bao gia si / lien he theo tan** (form hoac Zalo)
- Trang danh muc va trang chu phai noi duoc ca hai te: "ban le tu 1kg" va
  "bao gia cong trinh theo tan"
- Local SEO khong bo: `LocalBusiness` 2 dia diem (VP chinh Quan 5 + NHA MAY DINH tai
  CCN Lien Minh, Duc Hoa, LONG AN) + Google Business Profile - day la kenh cho te thau
