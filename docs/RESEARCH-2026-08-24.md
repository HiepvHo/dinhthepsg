## 3. He thong dong nghia quy cach [XAC THUC bang search that]

`5F` = `dinh 5p` = `dinh 5 phan` = `dinh 5cm` = `dinh 50mm`. **1 phan = 1cm = 10mm.**

Xac nhan cheo tren nhieu trang thuong mai doc lap (manhtienphat.vn, baogiathep.net,
tonthepvuongquankhoi.com) deu viet dang "5p (50mm)". Ten file anh cua doi thu
`Dinh-Chi-5f.jpg` xac nhan ky hieu F.

Thu tu nguoi ta go, suy ra tu do phu cua trang thuong mai (KHONG phai search volume):
`Np` > `N phan` > `Ncm` > `NF` > `Nmm`. Dang `Np` la dang pho bien nhat.

Cum ten de nham, phai lam ro ngay tren trang: dinh chi / dinh coffa / dinh coppha /
dinh sat / dinh dong go / dinh xay dung. Doi thu #1 dat ten trang ghep hai cach goi:
"dinh chi coffa 5p" - day la nuoc di dung, nen hoc.

Can kiem lai truoc khi viet: co trang ghi "5 phan = 1.27cm". 1.27cm dung bang 0.5 inch,
nen rat co the trang do dang noi trong ngu canh inch. **Doc nguyen van trang do truoc
khi xay mot khoi phan bien**, tranh dung straw man.

## 4. NHUNG GI CHUA XAC THUC - khong duoc dung lam co so uu tien

Critic doi khang bat duoc. Ghi lai de khong tu lua minh:

1. **KHONG CO BAT KY SO SEARCH VOLUME NAO.** Khong chay Keyword Planner, Ahrefs,
   DataForSEO, ke ca autocomplete voi geo=VN. Con so "950 truy van long-tail" la **BIA**.
   Xoa khoi moi ban ke hoach.
2. **Chi CO MOT SERP duoc xac thuc that**: "dinh chi 5p", do user quan sat 1 lan,
   1 thiet bi, 1 vi tri, khong incognito, khong doi chieu mobile. 17 truy van con lai
   va toan bo cot "difficulty de/trung binh/kho" la agent tu xep - **khong phai du lieu**.
3. **Bang "so cay dinh / kg" la SO TINH, khong phai so dem.** Cong thuc
   `pi*r^2*L*7.85` coi than dinh la tru dac va BO QUA khoi luong mu (mu chiem khoang
   8-12% o co nho), nen so that PHAI THAP HON. Trong research con ton tai hai con so
   lech nhau 9% cho cung 5F (414 vs 370-390).
   **Tuyet doi khong publish so tinh duoi nhan "dem thuc te tai nha may".**
4. **Luan diem "2.8mm moi dung, doi thu ghi 3.0-3.4mm la sai" LA SAI LOGIC.**
   "Phan" chi dinh nghia CHIEU DAI. Duong kinh than cua cung mot chieu dai thay doi
   theo nha san xuat va theo soi thep dau vao (JIS N50 = 2.75mm; nhieu thi truong chay
   3.0-3.4mm cho dinh 50mm). Doi thu rat co the dang cong bo dung so CUA HO.
   Khac biet phai viet la "spec cua chung toi, kem dung sai, theo lo" - KHONG phai
   "ca nganh ghi sai". Viet kieu do khong the bao ve va moi doi thu phan don.
5. **AI Overview la quan sat MOT LAN, khong tai lap duoc** (khac theo account, session,
   thoi diem). Khong dat cuoc noi dung lon nao vao no.
6. **FAQPage rich result DA BI BO** [xac thuc changelog Google]: thong bao 08/05/2024,
   ngung hien 07/05/2026, xoa tai lieu 15/06/2026. `HowTo` cung da bo.
   Nhung `Q&A`, `Education Q&A` va `Dataset` **VAN CON** trong search gallery - dung nham.
   Van nen viet Q&A cho nguoi doc va cho AI, chi la dung ky vong co ngoi sao tren SERP.
7. `priceValidUntil` cua doi thu het han 2025-10-10 nhung gia VAN hien tren SERP thang
   08/2026 => day la **hygiene**, KHONG phai don bay canh tranh.
8. Loi the "ta so huu bang duong kinh than that" rat de bi copy: do la 10 con so trong
   mot bang HTML, doi thu copy trong mot buoi chieu. Va co rui ro doc nguoc: cong bo
   2.8mm ben canh 3.0mm cua doi thu co the bi hieu thanh "dinh mong hon, yeu hon".

## 6. Kien truc de xuat

**Central Entity:** dinh thep xay dung phan theo quy cach. Don vi la cap
{loai dinh} x {quy cach}, vi do dung la cach nguoi mua goi ten san pham:
"dinh chi 5p", "dinh vang 1F6".

**Quyet dinh lon nhat - moi quy cach mot trang rieng.** Can cu tu skill `seo-programmatic`:

| Cong Scaled Content Abuse (Google siet manh 2025) | Nguong | Ta |
|---|---|---|
| Can content audit truoc khi publish | 100+ trang | **42 - khong cham nguong** |
| Hard stop, can duyet thu cong | 500+ trang | khong |
| Noi dung doc nhat giua 2 trang cung mau | >= 30-40% | spec khac that (2.8mm/50mm vs 4.5mm/100mm) |
| Loai trang AN TOAN khi scale | "product pages voi unique specs, comparison data" | dung loai |
| Loai trang BI PHAT | "chi doi ten thanh pho trong text giong nhau" | phai tranh mad-libs |

Dieu kien kem theo, khong duoc bo:
- Moi trang qua duoc "standalone value test": trang nay co dang publish neu khong
  ton tai trang tuong tu nao khac?
- Publish theo lo 50-100 trang, theo doi index + rank 2-4 tuan roi moi mo rong
- Khong mad-libs: khong chi thay con so trong cung mot doan van

**Schema moi trang san pham:** `Product` + `Offer` + `BreadcrumbList` (doi thu KHONG co
BreadcrumbList - day la khe hoi de an) + `Organization` / `LocalBusiness` cho 2 dia diem
(VP chinh Quan 5 + nha may dinh CCN Lien Minh, Duc Hoa, Long An).

**KHONG lam AggregateRating gia. Khong bia review.** Dung copy nuoc di 5 sao / 1 review
cua trungnamcons.

   voi cung cong do. Chua ai chay phep so sanh nay.

4. **Phap ly / nhan dien.** Khong tim thay dau vet dang ky ten cong ty, khong MST tra

   KHONG BAO GIO SHIP.** Phai dinh nghia truoc: thieu gi thi van len song duoc.
